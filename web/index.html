Use a US BIOS (model 1 or 2) and a dump of your own disc. On the Quest, zip the disc folder (the .cue and every .bin) into one .zip and choose that. On a computer you can also select the .cue and all its .bin files together, or one .chd. They're copied into this browser once and stay on the device.

Each layer of the Sega's picture sits at its own distance: backgrounds at the back, sprites and text boxes in front. Drag the picture to tilt it; double-click to straighten.

0 scenes saved. Each capture keeps the picture, a colour map of its layers and a fingerprint so the game can recognise it later. These become the depth maps.
